Good to know

Questions, answered.

Where does Elizma travel?

Elizma is based in Langebaan and also serves the surrounding areas. Message her your address or suburb and she will confirm.

How much does it cost?

Price depends on the service and your hair. Elizma confirms the full price before she comes, so there are no surprises. Visits in Langebaan have no call-out fee. Outside Langebaan there is a R150 call-out fee.

What do I need to prepare?

A comfortable spot with a chair, a mirror if you have one, and a power point nearby. She brings the rest.
